Reference frames, methods and limitations

SANSRS is Saudi Arabia’s national spatial reference system, comprising KSA-GRF, KSA-VRF and KSA-GEOID. KSA-GRF17 coincides with ITRF2014/IGS14 at 2017.0 and co-moves with the stable Arabian Plate. A future fully kinematic service is an educational concept here.

A plate-fixed frame can retain local deformation. Semi-kinematic delivery reduces modeled observations to an agreed reference epoch; it is a delivery convention rather than proof of zero physical motion.

The supplied comparison applies the first-order, time-dependent position-vector rotation of Table 2.3 to the IGS14 coordinates, then subtracts the selected KSA-GRF reference. Local East, North and ellipsoidal Up use a GRS80 tangent basis. No orthometric-height or geoid conversion is performed.

SINEX observations end in 2017. Propagating its velocities to 2026 is extrapolation. Formal covariance does not include later equipment changes, unmodeled motion or uncertainty of the 2026 solution. Blank and A flags are displayed verbatim because the CRD does not define them.
